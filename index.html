FAQ

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

The beta isn't code-signed yet. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". The installer only comes from our official GitHub releases page.

Do my streams go through your servers?

No. Video goes straight from your PC to YouTube, Twitch or Facebook, so there's no extra delay and nothing is recorded by us.

What internet speed do I need?

About the bitrate times the number of platforms. For example, 4.5 Mbps × 3 platforms ≈ 14 Mbps upload.

How do updates work?

The app checks for new versions and installs them when you restart. You never need to download it again.

Can I stream at 240 fps?

Live platforms accept up to 60 fps. Recordings can use up to 240 fps.

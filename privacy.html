Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

INL MultiStream ("the App") is a Windows desktop application made by INL Dev Studio, Bengaluru, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App uses, why it uses it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Google account basics: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture and a unique user ID through Firebase Authentication. We use these to identify your account and your Pro status.
  • Subscription status: whether you have Pro, your plan and its expiry date, stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore).
  • Payment confirmations: payments are processed by Razorpay. We receive a confirmation and the payment ID only. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.

2. YouTube data (Google API Services)

If you choose "Connect YouTube account", the App requests the youtube.force-ssl permission so it can, only when you ask it to:

  • show your channel name and picture inside the App;
  • create, schedule and list your live broadcasts and live streams;
  • set a broadcast's title, description, privacy, category and thumbnail;
  • get the stream address needed to send your video to YouTube.

Your YouTube access token is stored only on your own computer and is never sent to our servers. We do not sell, share or use YouTube data for advertising. The App's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The App uses the YouTube API Services, and by connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

You can remove the App's access at any time in the App (Disconnect Account) or at Google security settings.

3. Data that stays on your computer

Stream keys, settings, scenes, chat messages, local logs and recordings are stored only on your PC. Your video and audio go straight from your computer to the platforms you choose (YouTube, Twitch, Facebook or custom RTMP servers). They never pass through our servers.

5. How we use information

We use it only to sign you in, provide Pro features, process payments, prevent abuse and provide support. We do not sell personal data, and we do not show ads.

6. Third-party services

Google Firebase (authentication, database, cloud functions), Google YouTube Data API, Razorpay (payments), GitHub (app downloads and updates), and the streaming platforms you connect. Each has its own privacy policy.

7. Data retention and deletion

Account and subscription records are kept while your account is active. To delete your account data, email us and we will remove it within 30 days. Uninstalling the App removes local data; you may also delete the "inl-multistream" folder in %APPDATA%.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to our database is restricted by security rules so users can read only their own status.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and you must meet each streaming platform's minimum age to use it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be announced in the App or on this page.
